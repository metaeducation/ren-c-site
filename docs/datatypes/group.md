datatype: group!
summary: 'list that evaluates expressions as an isolated group'
antiform: pack!
---

A list in parentheses, such as `(1 + 2)`.  Evaluating a group runs it and gives its result.  Historical Rebol called this PAREN!.

Its antiform is [PACK!](/docs/datatypes/pack/), which carries several results at once.
