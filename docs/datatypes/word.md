---

A name, such as `append` or `x`.  Evaluating a word gets its variable, or runs the function it holds.

Setting and getting are written with [CHAIN!](/docs/datatypes/chain/)s (`x:` and `:x`) rather than separate word types.

Its antiform is [LOGIC!](/docs/datatypes/logic/): the antiforms of the words `okay` and `null`.
