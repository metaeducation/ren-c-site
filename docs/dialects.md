# Dialects

A dialect is a little language made of Revolt values, with its own rules
for what those values mean.  The interpreter's own evaluator is one
interpretation of a block.  A dialect is another.

Each dialect here has a whole section of its own, with its own concepts and
reference pages.  Following a link into one switches the sidebar over to
that dialect's pages, and the top of its sidebar has a link back.

| Dialect | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [PARSE](/docs/dialects/parse/) | Matching patterns in strings, binaries and blocks, and pulling values out of what matched. |
